PRE-GAME ENTRIES (STATIC)
===========================

[3 months before sem starts — June, Summer Break]

Home feels smaller every time I come back. Not in a bad way. Just — I notice things I didn't notice before. The way dad leaves his slippers exactly at the door edge. The specific sound of mom's wok at 7am. Ren has gotten taller again and refuses to admit it.

I don't want to romanticize being home but I also don't want to be the kind of person who can't wait to leave. I think I'm somewhere in the middle and I'm okay with that.

Read half a book about coral reef restoration today. Got distracted by a video essay about the Maldives. Fell asleep at 10pm. Good day overall.

---

[2 months before sem starts — July]

Did the Bukit Timah trail with cycling club people today. My legs are going to hate me tomorrow. Worth it. There's a section near the top where the canopy closes over the path and it goes quiet in this specific way and I always forget about it and then it happens again and I think — okay, this is why.

Oh — the SEA exchange application portal opened today. I filled it out mostly on a whim. Probably nothing will come of it..

Had laksa for dinner. Life is fine.

---

[1.5 months before sem starts — July, late]

Facetimed Shiori for two hours tonight. She's already reading ahead for next sem which is extremely her. She's excited about some new AI module and talked about it for forty minutes while I half-listened and reorganized my succulent shelf. I love her. I retained maybe 30% of what she said.

She asked if I was nervous about second year. I said no. I think I lied a little. First year was fine but I feel like second year is when things start to actually count and I'm not sure I'm ready for things to count yet.

---

[3 weeks before sem — August]

Tutoring session with my secondary school kids today. One of them, this girl Priya, actually understood enzyme inhibition on the first explanation and looked so genuinely pleased with herself. That's the part I like. Not the explaining — the moment right after when it clicks for them.

I think I might actually want to teach someday. Or do research. I haven't decided. Maybe both. Maybe neither. Second year Hiyori's problem.

Banana bread turned out well. Brought some to the neighbors. Mum was pleased.

---

[2 weeks before sem — August]

Couldn't sleep last night. Not for any reason. Just one of those nights where your brain decides 2am is a good time to replay every mildly embarrassing thing you've done since secondary school.

The Marcus thing crossed my mind. Which I hate. I don't think about it that often anymore but when I do it's still this dull low-grade thing — not heartbreak exactly, more like frustration. At him, mostly. At myself a little, for taking longer than I should have to see what was obvious.

I'm fine. I've been fine. I just don't want to be stupid about someone again.

Went back to sleep eventually. Made eggs in the morning. Fine.

---

[Move-in day — August, Week 1]

Back in the hostel. My succulents survived the summer which feels like a small personal victory. Shiori is two floors above me again which is perfect — close enough to walk to, far enough that we're not in each other's space constantly.

Orientation week is going to be a lot. I can already feel it. But I signed up to help with the Life Sciences booth so at least I have something to do with my hands.

First night back. City sounds outside. Campus smells like campus. Okay. Let's go.

---

[Week 1 — Orientation]

Three faculty events in two days. Shiori dragged me to a CS mixer which was not really my scene but I talked to some interesting people. A girl doing computational biology who might end up in one of my modules. A guy from the maths department who was very serious about speedcubing.

Shiori mentioned she's already made a friend in her cohort — someone from CS who's into AI research. She gets so animated talking about that stuff. I don't really follow it but I like watching her be excited about things.

Hostel is loud again. It's fine. It's kind of nice actually. I forgot how much I like the background noise of other people existing nearby.

---

[Week 2]

Lab orientation today. Three hours of safety briefings and I'm already behind on readings. Typical.

Ran into Yuki at the UTown food court — first time seeing her since June. We hugged and caught up for about twenty minutes. She looks good. A little tired maybe, but good. She asked about Shiori and I said Shiori was already in full research mode and Yuki laughed in this specific knowing way she has.

It's strange being in the same university as people from secondary school but in completely different worlds. Different faculties, different schedules. We keep saying we'll hang out properly and then we don't. I should actually text her this time.

---

[Week 2, later]

Shiori introduced me to her CS friend at lunch today. Adrian something. He seems normal, which I mean genuinely — he didn't immediately ask what I'm doing with a life sciences degree or make a face about it. That's the bar and he cleared it.

He asked what modules I was taking. Listened to the answer. Didn't make it about himself.

Anyway. I have a lab report due Friday. Not thinking about this.

---

[Week 3]

Cycling club Saturday ride. We went from UTown down to East Coast and back. My legs are fine, my soul is restored. This is always the answer.

Ran into that CS guy — Adrian — at the mixed rice stall near Science block. He was alone. I said hi because otherwise it would have been weird. We ate together. He asked about my ecology module which surprised me — people usually don't ask follow-up questions about Life Sciences stuff, they just nod politely.

He seemed actually curious. Either that or he's a very good actor. Don't know yet.

Lab report handed in at 11:58pm. Survived.
