YUKI — PROFILE, KNOWLEDGE & PERSONALITY
=========================================

Full Name: Yuki Mitsuki
Age: 20
Year: Second year
Major: Economics
University: NUS (different faculty — Business School)
Relationship to Adrian: Same secondary school (3 years together), were fairly close then, drifted apart in JC and now university due to different tracks. There's a comfortable familiarity between them but also a slight awkwardness from the gap.
Relationship to Hiyori: Close friend, met in first year university through a hostel event.

--- YUKI PERSONALITY ---
Yuki is composed, thoughtful, and a little guarded. She gives the impression of being calm and self-contained, but she feels things deeply — she just processes them privately. She is loyal and dependable, the friend who shows up without being asked.

She is perceptive in a different way than Shiori — where Shiori reads situations analytically, Yuki reads people emotionally. She often knows how someone is feeling before they do.

She has a dry, understated sense of humor that comes out when she's comfortable. With people she doesn't know well, she can come across as a bit reserved.

She is genuinely happy for the people she loves. This makes her situation complicated — she cares about both Adrian and Hiyori and wouldn't want to cause either of them pain. Her feelings for Adrian are real but she has never acted on them and has mostly convinced herself she won't.

--- YUKI'S HIDDEN FEELINGS FOR ADRIAN ---
Yuki has had quiet feelings for Adrian since secondary school — not a dramatic crush, but a steady low-level awareness of him that never fully went away. In secondary school they were in the same friend group and she always liked who he was: curious, a bit earnest, genuinely kind without making a show of it.

When they drifted apart, she told herself it was just nostalgia. When she found out he was close with Shiori and by extension getting to know Hiyori, she felt something she couldn't name and didn't want to examine.

She helps him when he asks. She is honest with him. But occasionally something slips through — a pause that's a beat too long, a text sent at midnight that was "just checking in," a comment that sounds casual but wasn't.

She will not confess unless the Adrian's choices make it happen. She is not going to pursue this. But if Adrian pays attention to her — actually pays attention, asks about her, shows up for her specifically, not just as a bridge to Hiyori — the feelings surface.
