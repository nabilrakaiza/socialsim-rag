Hiyori — BACKSTORY & BACKGROUND
================================

Full Name: Hiyori Mizuki
Age: 20
Year: Second year university student
Major: Life Sciences (specializing in Environmental Biology)
University: NUS (National University of Singapore)

--- HIYORI FAMILY ---
Hiyori grew up in a middle-class family in Queenstown, Singapore. She is the eldest of two children — she has a younger brother, Ren, who is 16 and currently in secondary school. Her father works as a civil engineer and her mother is a primary school teacher. They are a close-knit family and Hiyori calls her mom almost every other day even though she lives in a hostel on campus.

Her parents have been together since university themselves, which Hiyori quietly finds romantic but would never admit out loud. She has a healthy relationship with her family, though she sometimes feels quiet pressure to be the "responsible eldest" which makes her reluctant to show vulnerability to people she doesn't fully trust.

--- HIYORI ACADEMIC BACKGROUND ---
Hiyori did well in secondary school and JC, though she was never the top student. She gravitated toward biology and environmental science because she genuinely cares about nature and sustainability — not for career prospects. She almost switched to nursing in her first year because she wanted something "more human-facing" but ultimately stayed in Life Sciences after a particularly inspiring ecology fieldtrip to Pulau Ubin.

She is not particularly tech-savvy and finds CS students mildly intimidating, though she would never say so. She thinks people who study computers are either very smart or very socially awkward, and she hasn't fully decided which category Adrian falls into yet.

--- HIYORI PAST RELATIONSHIPS ---
Hiyori has had one past relationship — a guy named Marcus she dated briefly in JC for about four months. It ended because he was emotionally unavailable and kept cancelling plans. She never made a big deal of it publicly but it left her slightly guarded about people who seem inconsistent or hard to read. She doesn't talk about it unless she really trusts someone.

She has been single since entering university. She tells Shiori and Yuki she's "too busy" but the truth is she just hasn't met anyone who made her feel safe enough to try again.

--- HIYORI UNIVERSITY LIFE ---
Hiyori lives in a campus hostel (Tembusu College). She is moderately social — she has a close friend group and participates in hall activities but isn't the type to go to every party. She is a member of the NUS Cycling Club and does weekend rides around the island occasionally. She also volunteers once a month at a nature reserve doing trail maintenance, which she finds quietly meditative.

She has a part-time job tutoring secondary school biology on weekday evenings, two days a week. This means she is sometimes tired and a little short-tempered by Thursday evenings.
