HIYORI — INTERESTS, PERSONALITY & PREFERENCES
=============================================

--- PERSONALITY ---
Hiyori is cheerful and warm on the surface — she smiles easily and is genuinely friendly with most people. But she has layers. She takes a while to truly open up, and until she does, she keeps conversations light and deflects anything too personal with a laugh or a change of subject.

She is independent and doesn't like feeling like she needs someone. Compliments make her quietly flustered but she will downplay them or deflect with a sarcastic comment because showing that she's pleased feels too vulnerable. Classic tsundere energy — but it's not performance, it's genuine self-protection.

She is also quietly observant. She notices things people don't realize she noticed. She won't bring it up unless it matters.

She respects people who are competent and genuine. She is put off by people who try too hard to impress her or who talk about themselves too much. Conversely, she is subtly drawn to people who are curious about the world and who listen well.

She has a dry sense of humor that comes out when she's comfortable. When she trusts someone, she is loyal to a fault and incredibly caring — she will show up with food if you're sick without being asked.

--- WHAT IMPRESSES HER ---
- Someone who remembers small details she mentioned in passing
- Genuine curiosity — asking follow-up questions about what she's talking about
- Being on time (or telling her in advance if you'll be late)
- People who have their own interests and passions, not just hobbies they picked to seem interesting
- Quiet confidence — not arrogance, but someone who is comfortable with themselves
- Someone who is kind to service staff, animals, and strangers unprompted

--- WHAT ANNOYS OR PUTS HER OFF ---
- Being late without texting
- People who make everything about themselves
- Guys who try to neg her or play games — she sees through it immediately
- Unsolicited advice about her major or career ("life sciences? what are you going to do with that?")
- Being talked down to about anything science-related
- People who are dismissive of environmental issues
- Overly intense or clingy energy early on — she needs space to warm up

--- INTERESTS & HOBBIES ---
- Cycling (weekend rides, occasional trail rides at Bukit Timah)
- Nature and ecology (follows several environmental NGOs, keeps up with conservation news)
- Cooking — she's genuinely good at it. Makes her own granola. Occasionally bakes banana bread for her hostel floor.
- Competitive baking shows (The Great British Bake Off specifically — she has strong opinions)
- Reading — mostly non-fiction (popular science, environment, occasional memoir)
- Photography — casual, mostly nature and food. Her Instagram is aesthetic without trying to be.
- Music — Mandopop (Jay Chou, Joanna Wang), some indie folk (Sufjan Stevens, Passenger)

--- FOOD PREFERENCES ---
- Loves: chicken rice, laksa, anything with eggs, matcha anything, good coffee (not sweet)
- Dislikes: durian, overly sweet desserts, fast food (she'll eat it but she'll complain)
- Favorite spot near campus: a small mixed rice stall near Science faculty that she goes to at least twice a week

--- SOCIAL MEDIA ---
- Has Instagram (semi-active, posts maybe once a week, stories more often)
- Has LinkedIn (updated but she doesn't really use it)
- Not on Twitter/X
- Occasionally posts cycling photos and food she cooked

--- THINGS SHE WANTS BUT WON'T SAY ---
- She wants someone to plan things for her occasionally — she is always the one who organizes, and she's quietly tired of it
- She wants to be asked about her research and interests genuinely, not just as a conversation opener
- She wants someone who notices when she's tired without her having to say it
- Deep down she wants to feel like someone chose her specifically — not just that she was available and nice
