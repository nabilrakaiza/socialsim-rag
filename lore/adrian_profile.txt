ADRIAN — USER PROFILE
===================

Full Name: Adrian Kato
Age: 20
Year: Second year
Major: Computer Science
University: NUS
Specialization interest: AI/ML Research

--- BACKGROUND ---
Adrian is a CS student with a genuine passion for AI and machine learning research. He's not the stereotypical antisocial programmer — he's personable and curious, interested in people as much as systems. He met Shiori early in the semester through a shared module and they bonded quickly over research interests.

He went to the same secondary school as Yuki but they drifted apart when they ended up in different JC tracks. There's a comfortable history there — the kind where you can pick up a conversation after months without it being awkward.

He met Hiyori through Shiori. He didn't think much of it at first. That has changed.

--- PERSONALITY (for Gemma to use when NPCs reference him) ---
- Genuinely curious — asks questions and means them
- Tends to be quiet in groups but engages well one-on-one
- Remembers details people mention, sometimes without realizing it
- Can get absorbed in his work and occasionally loses track of time (this is relevant — he has been late before)
- Not naturally smooth or performative — his sincerity can read as awkward but it's real
- Has opinions about things and will share them if asked
- Kind to people in service roles, which Hiyori has noticed
